Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

TubeTunes is a music player for YouTube playlists, available as a website and an Android app. This page explains what data TubeTunes uses and why.

What TubeTunes accesses

When you choose "Connect YouTube", you sign in with Google and grant TubeTunes the youtube.readonly permission. TubeTunes uses it only to read:

  • your YouTube channel name and profile picture, to show which account is connected;
  • the playlists on your channel and the videos in them;
  • the videos you have liked.

TubeTunes cannot post, delete, upload or change anything on your account.

Where your data is stored

Your library (song titles, playlists and the account name) is stored only on your own device: in your browser for the website, and on your phone for the app. TubeTunes has no server that receives or stores your data, and we do not sell or share it.

Google sign-in gives TubeTunes a temporary access token. On the website it is kept only until you close the browser tab. TubeTunes talks directly to Google's servers with it.

YouTube and Google

TubeTunes uses YouTube API Services and plays videos through YouTube's official embedded player. By using TubeTunes you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • Use "Disconnect YouTube" in TubeTunes to remove your YouTube playlists from the app.
  • Remove TubeTunes' access any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything TubeTunes stored.
